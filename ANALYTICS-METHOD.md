# Phase 6 Analytics Method

Phase 6 intentionally derives analytics from observable attempt records:

- **Score** = correct keyed questions / keyed questions.
- **Area accuracy** = correct answered keyed questions tagged to that CELE area / attempted keyed questions in that area.
- **Topic accuracy** follows the same rule at topic level.
- **Question pace** comes from per-question open time stored during navigation/submission.
- **Average score** is the mean of submitted attempts that have usable answer keys.
- **Best score** is the highest such submitted score.
- **Streak** counts consecutive calendar dates with submitted attempts.
- **Tomorrow's Review** ranks weakest observed topics/areas and adds a short mistake replay. It does not claim to predict CELE performance.

Missing tags or answer keys remain missing; the app does not manufacture analytics from them.
